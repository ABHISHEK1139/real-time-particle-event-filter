# Unsupervised Anomaly Detection Benchmark

> Evaluates Isolation Forest trained exclusively on continuum background kinematics
> without invariant mass labels. Models new physics resonant discovery at trigger level.

**Target Background Acceptance Budget**: 5.0%

| Model | Training Paradigm | Signal Efficiency @5% bg | AUROC | AUPRC |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Isolation Forest | Unsupervised (Background only) | **98.74%** | 0.9935 | 0.8424 |

TEST Background Retained: 4.99% (Target: 5.0%).

> Conclusion: Without ever seeing an invariant mass label, the unsupervised detector isolates 98.74% of Z-boson candidate events at 5% false positive rate.
